Story maker

Each line of the story is shown on the camera's 7 segment cells at the bottom of the display: 2 on ISO, 2 on F and 3 on SS. The line your cursor is on is shown below. Click a segment to change the glyph for that character; the glyphs are listed at the bottom of the page, where + / − (on hover) spreads a glyph over two cells, like W. A . right after the 3rd character lights the F decimal point and takes no cell.

Font

Click segments to edit a glyph. A blue dot marks a changed glyph; an orange character looks the same as another one (hover for which). Changes are kept in this browser. The camera's font is story/font.json: paste the copied JSON there and rebuild (SILENT=1 sh tools/build.sh). Only ASCII characters can go in the firmware.
